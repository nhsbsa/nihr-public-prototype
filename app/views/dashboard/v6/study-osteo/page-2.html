Osteoarthritis Pain Management & Mobility Study

Help researchers evaluate a new digital tool designed to support people living with osteoarthritis in managing joint pain and staying active.

Online Birmingham

What you will do

You will have a 20-minute telephone screening call to check your eligibility. If eligible, you will attend one in-person visit at the Birmingham Hub (lasting up to 1 hour) where we will show you how to use the digital tool and ask you to complete a baseline mobility questionnaire.

After that, you will use the tool at home for 12 weeks and complete two 30-minute online follow-up sessions.

Incentives and reimbursement

You will not be paid for taking part, but your travel costs to and from the Birmingham Regional Hub will be fully reimbursed.

Number of visits

One in-person clinic visit and two online sessions.
