About the study

A team of researchers and clinicians are carrying out a study to evaluate a new digital self-management tool for people living with osteoarthritis. We know that regular movement and pain tracking can help improve symptoms, but many people find it difficult to stay active. This study aims to see if our new app can support people in managing their joint pain and improving their daily mobility.

Study location

The study is mostly online, but includes an initial visit to the Birmingham Regional Hub.

Birmingham Regional Hub
12 Broad Street
Birmingham
B1 2EA

Who can participate in the study?

You may be able to take part in this study if:

  • you are aged 50 to 85 years old
  • you have been diagnosed with osteoarthritis in your knee or hip
  • you own a smartphone or tablet to use the digital tool
  • you are not currently taking part in another clinical trial
