Who can take part?

  • You are aged between 50 and 85
  • You have been diagnosed with early-stage cognitive impairment
  • You have a family member, partner or friend who can help confirm any changes
  • You live in the UK

You cannot take part if:

  • You have been diagnosed with a different type of dementia
  • You are currently taking part in another drug trial
  • You cannot attend a Birmingham regional hub for a follow-up visit
