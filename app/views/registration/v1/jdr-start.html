Registering to Join Dementia Research

We need some details so that we can match you with relevant research studies.

Registering is voluntary and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Your medical care will not be affected if you withdraw from research.

By registering, you are giving consent for researchers, NHS staff and partner organisations to access your medical records.

Registering someone else for dementia research

You can only register another person for dementia research if:

  • they have mental capacity and they have consented to be part of research
  • you have Lasting Power of Attorney for health and wellfare (Welfare Power of Attorney in Scotland)
