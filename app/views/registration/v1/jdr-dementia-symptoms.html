{% call details({ summaryText: "What are mild symptoms of dementia?" }) %}

Mild symptoms of dementia include:

  • Loss of memory for recent events
  • Repeating yourself
  • Becoming slower to grasp new ideas
  • Finding it harder to make decisions
  • Showing signs of confusion
{% endcall %} {% call details({ summaryText: "What are moderate symptoms of dementia?" }) %}

Moderate symptoms of dementia include:

  • Needing more help with day-to-day living
  • Needing frequent reminders or help with eating, washing, dressing and using the toilet
  • Becoming increasingly forgetful and sometimes repeating the same question or phrase
  • Failing to recognise familiar people or confusing them with others
{% endcall %} {% call details({ summaryText: "What are severe symptoms of dementia?" }) %}

Severe symptoms of dementia include:

  • Being unable to recognise familiar objects, people or surroundings and pronounced memory loss
  • Needing increased help with day-to-day activities
  • Becoming more dependent on others for care needs
  • Becoming increasingly frail and starting to shuffle or walk unsteadily
